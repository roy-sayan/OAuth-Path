How to read this

Why this exists. With Composio, some toolkits already have a managed OAuth app, so users connect via a Connect Link and you register nothing. For the rest, or when you want your own branding, custom scopes or your own rate-limit quota, you register an OAuth app with the provider and give Composio its client ID and secret. That is where weeks can disappear. Check which case you are in first: composio.toolkits.get("slug").composio_managed_auth_schemes, or the managed OAuth directory.

Evidence labels. Primary read in provider or vendor docs. Secondary only seen in third-party write-ups. Unverified not checked or likely to change.

What was and was not tested. The four library guides were built from web research on 29 Oct 2026. No provider accounts were created and no approvals were attempted, so every "time" is an estimate from documentation and reports, not a measurement.
